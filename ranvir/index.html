Liam Ranvir
The Xenoglossy

About -

Once the leader of his tried-and-true friends in the Queensguard.
Now on his own quest for self-discovery and adventure.
Wanderlust weighs heavily on his mind, but the encounters he'll have and stories he'll write along the way will make the journey worthwhile.
Regret and sadness, however, loom ever closer as constant reminders of his failures.

Current Pursuits -

Destination: Unknown.
Chance encounters and fleeting moments have yet to take on greater significance.

The Xenoglossy -

The title of Liam's combat occupation.
The Xenoglossy imbues their weapons with both light and dark variations of elemental magicks.
This strength is derived from their memories and connections with individuals.

Skills and spells

skills
& spells

xenoglossy artes

spell passive All basic elemental spells take on properties based on which alignment Liam has swapped to. Light will typically spread and provide AoE traits to magic, while Darkness converges magic into focused points of deadly precision.

elemental arsenal

spell passive Fire, Water, Wind, and Earth magicks heed Liam's call. While he has yet to master any of them, their versatility, combined with his Xenoglossy talents, gives him a formidable toolkit for a variety of encounters and situations.

chain of memories

spell active The Mark of the Xenoglossy empowers its bearer with the memories and connections they've made. Liam is capable of actively switching his 'Chain of Memories' to specific individuals of import in his life, thereby strengthening his magicks and often granting him new abilities. The effects of the current Chain will remain until swapped.

vermillion flare

spell active Combining all elemental magicks within his arsenal, Liam unleashes a chaotic storm of focused fury upon an enemy, scorching and tearing them asunder. Usable only under the most dire of conditions and circumstances, this will leave him drained for a significant amount of time afterward.

blade logic

skill passive Liam adopts a dueling style of combat most of the time, though he can handle himself just as well when the odds are stacked against him. Of his preferred weapons, he uses swords and spears most often.

blade logic: rend

skill active The first branch of his teachings, and the most powerful thanks to his constant practice. Liam attacks with his aligned elements and sends shockwaves from his weapon that burst forth from the point of impact. Particularly useful for armored foes.

blade logic: sever

skill active The second branch of blade logic. Liam can send projectiles from his weapon based on his chosen elemental alignment. The effects vary dramatically between alignments.

Inventory and out of character

inventory.

fehlgrace.

Refined, reforged, and remade countless times. A lightweight bastard sword that has endured time and countless trials. The unique meteoric alloy it was crafted from allows it to hold its edge almost indefinitely. It becomes even deadlier once imbued with Liam's magicks as a Xenoglossy.

a saint's ring.

A promise made tangible. Often worn as a pendant around Liam's neck. It acts as a communication device, allowing him to speak to Saint at certain moments.

traveller's pack.

A curious bag that can disappear and reappear on command whenever Liam wills it. Otherwise, it's used normally like any other pack. A steadfast traveling companion for any adventurer.
